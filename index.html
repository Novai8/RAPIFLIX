Rapiflix is an independent film discovery and playback project. The free library resolves compatible video files from Internet Archive items and plays them inside Rapiflix. Commercial titles open official or configured viewing options where available.

Movie metadata is provided by TMDB. Rapiflix uses TMDB APIs but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Source availability and rights depend on the underlying provider.
